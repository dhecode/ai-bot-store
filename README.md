# BotForge AI Agency Storefront

Your complete, ready-to-deploy storefront where clients can test live bots and purchase AI automation packages.

## How to View and Test It Locally:
1. Open your browser and drag & drop `index.html` into it, or open `file:///C:/Users/lenovo/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/ai-bot-store/index.html`.
2. Try clicking the **USD / INR** currency toggle in the top bar.
3. Test drive the **Live Interactive Chat Simulator** by switching between:
   - Car Detailing & Workshop
   - Luxury Salon & Tattoo Studio
   - Real Estate Broker
4. Click **"Order Voice Receptionist"** to see the instant WhatsApp ordering modal.

## How to Connect Your Real WhatsApp Number:
Open [index.html](file:///C:/Users/lenovo/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/ai-bot-store/index.html) and go to **line 377**:
Change:
```javascript
const whatsappNumber = "919999999999";
```
to your own WhatsApp number with country code (e.g. `919876543210` or `1234567890`).

## How to Host It Online in 60 Seconds (100% Free):
1. Go to [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) or [Vercel](https://vercel.com).
2. Simply drag and drop this `ai-bot-store` folder.
3. You get a live link like `https://botforge-ai.netlify.app` that you can immediately put in your:
   - YouTube video description
   - Instagram / TikTok bio
   - Direct messages to local business owners
